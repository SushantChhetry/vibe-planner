# FAQ

Quick answers about accounts, data, and what PuMi can export—see **[How-tos](/help/how-tos)** for step-by-step workflows.

## Do I need an account?

Yes. Sign in to create projects and save canvas, site map, and wireframe data. Without a session, you cannot persist work in PuMi.

## Where is my data stored?

Projects, pages, blocks, and related canvas data are stored in a **Supabase** database tied to your workspace and account. Nothing is stored only in your browser for long-term use—you need a connection so the app can load and save.

## What is the difference between the free and Premium plans?

- **Free** — **One** project; **Markdown**, **`.cursorrules`**, **`CLAUDE.md`**, and **JSON** export; core site map and page canvas with wireframes.
- **Premium** — **More projects**; **AI blueprint** templates, canvas **appearance** options, and **team**-related features where available.

Check current limits anytime on the **[dashboard](/dashboard)** and upgrade from **[Settings → Billing](/settings/billing)**.

## What can I export?

Open **Export** on the canvas toolbar. You can generate **Markdown**, **`.cursorrules`** (Cursor), **`CLAUDE.md`**-style context (Claude), or **JSON**. Exports include pages, blocks, navigation between pages, and wireframe detail when you have drawn it.

## Can I use PuMi offline?

No. You need a network connection to load the app and sync saves. If you lose connectivity, wait until you are back online before relying on unsaved changes.

## How do undo and redo work?

**Undo** and **Redo** apply on a **page** design tab (blocks and wireframes for that screen), not on the **Site map**. Use the footer buttons, or **⌘Z** / **⌘⇧Z** (Mac) or **Ctrl+Z** / **Ctrl+Shift+Z** (Windows/Linux) when focus is not inside a text field. See **[How-tos → Undo and history](/help/how-tos#undo-and-history)**.

## Who can I contact for help?

Start with this **[Help](/help)** hub and **[How-tos](/help/how-tos)**. For **billing or plan changes**, use **[Billing](/settings/billing)**. For product bugs or feature requests, use whatever channel your deployment uses (e.g. issue tracker or team email)—PuMi has no in-app ticket queue by default.
